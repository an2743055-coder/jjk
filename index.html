Architecture · How It Works

From data to decision, in four moves.

A transparent pipeline you can audit at any stage. No black boxes, no untraceable outputs — every response is grounded in a citable source.

01
Ingest

Connect your knowledge base

We pull from Confluence, Notion, Slack, your help center, PDFs, and internal APIs — chunking intelligently by document structure, not arbitrary character counts.

confluence notion slack pdf api
02
Embed

Vectorize for semantic search

Documents become searchable embeddings in a vector database — pgvector, Pinecone, or Qdrant — with hybrid retrieval combining dense vectors and BM25 keyword matching for precision.

pgvector pinecone qdrant hybrid
03
Retrieve

Pull relevant context, rank, rerank

When a query arrives, the system retrieves the top-K chunks, reranks them with a cross-encoder, and assembles a grounded prompt — complete with citations the bot will reference in its answer.

cross-encoder rerank citations top-k
04
Respond

Generate, act, or escalate

The LLM generates a grounded response. If the query needs action, an agentic layer triggers tool calls — refunding a charge, updating a ticket, escalating to a human when confidence drops below threshold.

tool-use escalation guardrails audit-log

Trust & Security

Enterprise-grade by default.

Your data never trains a public model. Every deployment is isolated, encrypted, and auditable — deployable in your VPC, on-premise, or in our managed cloud.

End-to-end encryption

AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, with per-tenant encryption keys rotated on a 90-day schedule.

On-premise deployment

Ship the entire stack to your own VPC or bare-metal infrastructure. Your data, your network, your perimeter.

SOC 2 Type II

Annual third-party audits covering security, availability, confidentiality, and processing integrity.

Zero data retention

Queries and responses are purged on configurable schedules. No training on your data, ever — written into our DPA.

Role-based access

Granular RBAC down to the document level — agents only retrieve what each user is authorized to see.

Full audit logs

Every retrieval, generation, and tool call is logged with timestamps, inputs, and outputs — exportable to your SIEM.

Compliance-ready out of the box

GDPR, HIPAA, and CCPA aligned. Data residency options for EU, US, and APAC regions.
